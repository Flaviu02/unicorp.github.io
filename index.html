What’s the trouble?

If you recognize any of these, we can help. If yours isn’t listed, call anyway.

  • “My computer has become very slow.”We find out why, clean it up, and explain in plain words what we did.
  • “I got an email or call that seems wrong.”We check it with you, remove anything harmful, and show you how to spot the next one.
  • “I can’t get into my email or account.”We get you back in and set things up so it doesn’t happen again.
  • “The printer, Wi-Fi or TV won’t connect.”We track down the cause and get everything working together.
  • “I just bought a new computer or tablet.”We set it up, move over your photos and files, and show you the basics.
  • “I’d like to learn how to do something.”Video calls with family, online banking, sharing photos. One-on-one, at your pace.
